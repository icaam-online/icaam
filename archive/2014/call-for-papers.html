Call for Papers

SHORT ABSTRACTS

Abstracts and conference program will be published in a conference booklet. Procedure of the submission of abstract paper is via online submission system. A sample abstract is given below. The deadline of short abstract submission is July 15th, 2014. To submit a short abstract, please register and use the online system. Sample abstract file: Word Guide.

EXTENDED ABSTRACTS

Each participant must submit a short abstract. The deadline for submission of extended abstracts for publication in the AIP is June 1st, 2014. Authors who want to publish their extended abstracts (4-6 pages) in AIP Proceedings Series should prepare their articles in LaTeX with single-column 8.5 x 11 template format. In addition to conference registration fee, authors should pay an extra fee ($80) per article. Extended abstracts should be submitted to icaammail@gmail.com. Sample tex (icaam_template.tex) and pdf (icaam_template.pdf) files are available for download.

FULL PAPERS

The selected full papers of this conference will be published in the following peer-reviewed journals:
Boundary Value Problems (http://www.boundaryvalueproblems.com) - Topical Series: Recent Advances in Partial Differential Equations and Their Applications
Contemporary Analysis and Applied Mathematics (http://caam.fatih.edu.tr)

Preliminary discussions are being carried out for a special issue of Numerical Functional Analysis and Optimization (http://www.tandfonline.com/loi/lnfa20). According to the scope of the journals the papers will be directed to them. Full paper submission will be done via journals' online submission pages.
