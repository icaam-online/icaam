Call for Papers

SHORT ABSTRACTS

Abstracts and conference program will be published in a conference booklet. Procedure of the submission of abstract paper is during the registration. A sample abstract is given below. The deadline of short abstract submission is August 1st, 2016. Sample Abstract File: template.pdf, template.tex.

AIP PAPERS (EXTENDED ABSTRACTS)

Each participant must submit a short abstract. The deadline for submission of extended abstracts for publication in the AIP is May 1st, 2016. Authors who want to publish their extended abstracts (4-6 pages) in AIP Proceedings Series should prepare their articles in LaTeX with single-column 8.5 x 11 template format. In addition to conference registration fee, authors should pay an extra fee ($80) per article. Extended abstracts should be submitted online.

FULL PAPERS - FILOMAT (http://journal.pmf.ni.ac.rs/filomat)

Special Issue on Recent Advances in Pure and Applied Mathematics (Deadline for submissions: 10 December 2016). Filomat is a free mathematics journal of the Faculty of Science and Mathematics at the University of Nis, Serbia, indexed in the Science Citation Index. The special issue is dedicated to high-quality research papers presented in ICAAM-2016 in the fields of pure and applied mathematics and their applications. Potential topics include: Operator theory; Asymptotical Methods; Geometry of Banach spaces; Summability; Statistical summability and statistical approximation; Sequence spaces and fuzzy sequence spaces; Topological properties of the matrix domains; Harmonic analysis; Numerical functional analysis and applications; Computational methods in PDEs; Equations of gas and hydrodynamics; Local and nonlocal boundary value problems for PDE; Reaction-Diffusion equations and systems; Identification problems; Control problems; Quantitative theory of differential equations.

FULL PAPERS - Contemporary Analysis and Applied Mathematics (http://caam.fatih.edu.tr)

CAAM is an international journal dedicated to the latest advancement in analysis and applied mathematics. All manuscripts must be prepared in English and are subject to a rigorous and fair peer-review process. The journal is indexed in several databases such as MathSciNet, Zentralblatt MATH, and VINITI. Submissions must be made through the journal online system. Manuscripts must be prepared in LaTeX; a sample TeX file is available from the journal.
