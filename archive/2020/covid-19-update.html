COVID-19 Update

We hope all is well with you and your family in these stressful times. Please stay safe and take care.

We want to update you about the conference. Since April 18, no new cases have been reported in Girne (Kyrenia) and Northern Cyprus. The flights do not return to normal schedule yet and we do not have an update on this. The Organizing Committee decided to continue all publication processes as scheduled and we plan to meet on-site when everything becomes safe. We will work out the details and inform you about the exact dates of the conference as soon as possible.

SUMMARY OF IMPORTANT DATES

  • AIP Conference Proceedings: the submission of extended abstracts was extended; the new deadline is August 17, 2020.
  • Full paper submission to Bulletin of the Karaganda University-Mathematics: deadline July 1, 2020 (closed; Special Issue published online).
  • Full paper submission to International Journal of Applied Mathematics (IJAM): deadline December 26, 2020.
  • Full paper submission to e-Journal of Analysis and Applied Mathematics: open for submissions.

Best regards, ICAAM 2020 Organizing Committee.
