SHORT ABSTRACTS

Abstracts and conference program will be published in a conference booklet. The procedure of the submission of abstract paper is via the online submission system. First complete your registering and submit your short abstract. Please upload the short abstract as a .TeX file. A sample abstract is given below. The deadline for short abstract submission is August 17, 2020. The ABSTRACT book of ICAAM 2020 was published.

AIP PAPERS (EXTENDED ABSTRACTS) - Paper submission is closed

Each participant must submit a short abstract before submitting AIP Paper. The deadline for submission of extended abstracts for publication in the AIP is August 17, 2020. Authors who want to publish their extended abstracts (4-6 pages) in AIP Proceedings Series should prepare their articles in LaTeX with single-column 8.5 x 11 template format. AIP Publishing has made a formatted template available in Overleaf. In addition to conference registration fee, authors should pay an extra fee ($90) per article. Please send an email to icaammail@gmail.com to inform about payment. You can pay using PayPal: https://www.paypal.me/icaam

FULL PAPERS - Bulletin of the Karaganda University-Mathematics (https://mathematics-vestnik.ksu.kz/)

Paper submission is closed. Deadline for submissions: July 1, 2020. The Special Issue was published online. The journal is included in the Emerging Sources Citation Index from Web of Science Core Collection of Clarivate Analytics. The present issue (No.3 for 2020) is devoted to high-quality research papers presented in ICAAM-2020. Guest Editors: Allaberen Ashyralyev (aallaberen@gmail.com), Near East University, Turkey; Makhmud Sadybekov (sadybekov@math.kz), Institute of Math. and Math. Modeling, Kazakhstan.

FULL PAPERS - International Journal of Applied Mathematics (IJAM) (http://www.diogenes.bg/ijam), Scopus indexed

Deadline for submissions: 26 December 2020. Special Issue on "Recent Advances in PDE and Their Applications". The present special issue is devoted to high-quality research papers presented in ICAAM-2020 in the fields of construction and investigation of solutions of well-posed and ill-posed boundary value problems for partial differential equations and their related applications. Potential topics include: problems involving positive operators with classical and nonclassical boundary conditions; equations of gas and hydrodynamics; new methods in theory and applications of PDEs; stochastic PDE models and applications; computational methods in PDEs; fractional differential equations and applications; identification problems; quantitative theory of differential equations; control problems. Guest Editors: Allaberen Ashyralyev (allaberen.ashyralyev@neu.edu.tr); Virginia Kiryakova (virginia@diogenes.bg).

An international journal dedicated to the latest advancements in analysis and applied mathematics.
