Call for Papers

SHORT ABSTRACTS

Abstracts and conference program will be published in a conference booklet. Procedure of the submission of abstract paper is via online submission system. First complete your registering and submit your short abstract. Please upload short abstract as .TeX file. A sample abstract is given below. The deadline of short abstract submission is August 1, 2018.

FULL PAPERS - Mathematical Modelling of Natural Phenomena (https://www.mmnp-journal.org)

Special Issue on Spectral Problems in Mathematical Modelling. Deadline for submissions: September 15, 2018; Deadline of presentation for publication: November 24, 2018. The present special issue is devoted to the publication of high-quality research papers presented in ICAAM-2018 in the fields of the spectral problems in mathematical modeling and their related applications.

  • Guest Editors:
  • Allaberen Ashyralyev, aallaberen@gmail.com, Near East University, Turkey
  • Michael Ruzhansky, m.ruzhansky@imperial.ac.uk, Imperial College London, UK
  • Makhmud Sadybekov, sadybekov@math.kz, Institute of Math. and Math. Modeling, Kazakhstan

SPRINGER BOOK SERIES - Functional Analysis in Interdisciplinary Applications, II (https://www.springer.com/series/10533)

Submission date: December 1, 2018; Acceptance notification: February 1, 2019; Deadline for final papers: February 15, 2019. Proceedings of ICAAM 2018 will be published in the well-known book series Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics. All articles must contain new results and go through a refereeing process.

Preliminary sections (topics):
Theory of Functions and Functional Spaces
Differential Equations and Boundary Value Problems
Differential and Integral Operators
Mathematical methods in Physical Sciences
Computational Methods and Numerical Analysis

The authors must submit their papers to the email: icaam2018-springer@math.kz

FULL PAPERS - Bulletin of the Karaganda University-Mathematics (http://new.ksu.kz/en_matem.html)

Article submission is closed. Deadline for submissions: July 1, 2018. The journal is included in the Emerging Sources Citation Index from Web of Science Core Collection database of Clarivate Analytics. The present issue (No.3 for 2018) is devoted to the publication of high-quality research papers presented in ICAAM-2018.

  • Guest Editors:
  • Allaberen Ashyralyev, aallaberen@gmail.com, Near East University, Turkey
  • Makhmud Sadybekov, sadybekov@math.kz, Institute of Math. and Math. Modeling, Kazakhstan

AIP PAPERS (EXTENDED ABSTRACTS) - Submission is closed

Each participant must submit short abstract before submitting AIP Paper. The deadline for submission of extended abstracts for publication in the AIP is May 10, 2018. Authors who want to publish their extended abstracts (4-6 pages) in AIP Proceedings Series should prepare their articles in LaTeX with single-column 8.5 x 11 template format. In addition to conference registration fee, authors should pay an extra fee ($80) per article.

FULL PAPERS - e-Journal of Analysis and Applied Mathematics (https://content.sciendo.com/view/journals/ejaam/ejaam-overview.xml)

e-Journal of Analysis and Applied Mathematics is an international journal dedicated to the latest advancements in analysis and applied mathematics. Only original and unpublished articles are accepted; the decision on acceptance is taken after a peer-reviewing procedure. The journal makes no page charges. Submission of an article is via submission@ejaam.org
