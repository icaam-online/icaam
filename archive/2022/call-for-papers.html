AIP PAPERS (EXTENDED ABSTRACTS)

Each participant must submit a short abstract before submitting an AIP Paper. The deadline for submission of extended abstracts for publication in the AIP is November 18, 2022. Authors who want to publish their extended abstracts (4-6 pages) in AIP Proceedings Series should prepare their articles in LaTeX with single-column 8.5 x 11 template format. AIP Publishing has made a formatted template available in Overleaf. In addition to the conference registration fee, authors should pay an extra fee ($160) per article. Please send an email to icaammail@gmail.com to inform about payment. Payment can be made via PayPal: https://www.paypal.me/icaam
